About these numbers
Values
The Value and CovrPrice columns in your CLZ Comics Web export of Nov 8, 2026. They won't change until the app is republished with a new export.
Grading cost estimate
CGC's Modern tier fee is $30 per book from January 2026; the $50 here adds an allowance for shipping and insurance. Older books and higher-value tiers cost more.
Covers
From your CLZ Print to PDF report of Nov 8, 2026, matched to the export row by row. Comics with no cover in that report show a placeholder.
Left out
